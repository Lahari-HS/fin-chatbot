# Section 80C Tax Deductions Overview

Last reviewed: 2026-11-01
Note: Applicable under the Old Tax Regime. The New Tax Regime does not offer Section 80C deductions.

## Overview
Section 80C allows a deduction of up to **₹1,50,000** from gross total income in a financial year.

## Eligible Investment Instruments
1. **ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme)**: 3-year lock-in (shortest among 80C), equity market returns.
2. **PPF**: 15-year lock-in, government-guaranteed interest, tax-free returns.
3. **EPF / VPF**: Employee's contribution to PF.
4. **Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)**: For girl children below 10 years.
5. **Tax-saving 5-Year Bank FDs**: Locked for 5 years, interest is taxable.
6. **Life Insurance Premium**: Term plan or life policy premiums.
