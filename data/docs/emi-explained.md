# Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) Explained

Last reviewed: 2026-11-01
Note: Check with your lending institution (bank or NBFC) for amortisation schedules, processing fees, and reset clauses.

## What is an EMI?
An Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month. EMIs apply to both personal and secured loans (e.g., home loans, car loans).

## Anatomy of an EMI
Each EMI payment contains two components:
- **Principal component**: The portion that goes toward reducing the original loan balance.
- **Interest component**: The fee charged by the lender for borrowing the money.

In the early years of a long-term loan (like a 20-year home loan), interest constitutes the largest chunk of the EMI. As the outstanding principal shrinks, the interest portion decreases while the principal repayment increases.

## Prepayment Advantage
Prepaying principal early in the loan tenure drastically cuts total interest paid and can reduce the loan tenure significantly.
