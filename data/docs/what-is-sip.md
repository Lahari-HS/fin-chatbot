# Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)

Last reviewed: 2026-11-01
Note: Please verify terms and fund performance with SEBI or official AMC scheme documents.

## What is an SIP?
A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a method of investing a fixed amount regularly (typically monthly) into a mutual fund scheme, rather than investing a large lump sum all at once.

## Key Benefits
1. **Rupee Cost Averaging**: When markets are down, your fixed installment buys more mutual fund units; when markets rise, you buy fewer units. Over time, this smooths out your average purchase price.
2. **Power of Compounding**: Consistent reinvestment of returns allows your wealth to grow exponentially over long horizons (10-20+ years).
3. **Financial Discipline**: It automates savings directly from your bank account every month before discretionary spending occurs.

## Misconceptions
- An SIP is NOT an asset or mutual fund product itself; it is simply a disciplined mechanism or vehicle for buying mutual funds.
- SIP returns are market-linked and never guaranteed.
