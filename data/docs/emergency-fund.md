# Building an Emergency Fund

Last reviewed: 2026-11-01
Note: Guidelines vary based on job stability and dependents.

## What is an Emergency Fund?
An emergency fund is a safety cushion of liquid cash reserved strictly for unplanned life events, such as medical emergencies, sudden home repairs, or unexpected job loss.

## Recommended Target
- **Salaried / Dual Income**: 3 to 6 months of mandatory living expenses (rent, food, utility bills, EMIs, insurance premiums).
- **Freelancers / Self-Employed**: 9 to 12 months of expenses due to higher income volatility.

## Where to Keep It?
Never lock an emergency fund in equity mutual funds or volatile investments. Keep it split between:
1. High-yield savings bank account (instant access).
2. Liquid mutual funds or sweep-in bank Fixed Deposits (accessible within 24-48 hours).
