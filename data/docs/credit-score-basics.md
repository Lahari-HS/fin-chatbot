# Understanding Your CIBIL Credit Score

Last reviewed: 2026-11-01
Note: Check your credit report at least once a year through official credit bureaus (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF).

## Score Range
- **750 – 900 (Excellent)**: Preferred interest rates, fast loan approvals.
- **700 – 749 (Good)**: Likely to get approved with standard terms.
- **650 – 699 (Fair)**: May face higher interest rates or stricter collateral demands.
- **Below 650 (Poor)**: High rejection rate for unsecured cards and loans.

## How to Improve Your Score
1. Pay all credit card balances and loan EMIs on or before the due date without fail.
2. Maintain credit utilization below 30% of total limit.
3. Avoid applying for multiple new credit cards or loans simultaneously.
4. Keep older credit accounts open to establish a long credit history.
