# Public Provident Fund (PPF), EPF, and NPS Basics

Last reviewed: 2026-11-01
Note: Rules and interest rates are subject to Government of India quarterly revisions.

## 1. Public Provident Fund (PPF)
- **Tenure**: 15 years (extendable in 5-year blocks).
- **Tax Status**: EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt) — investment, interest, and maturity are completely tax-free under Section 80C.
- **Maximum Limit**: ₹1.5 lakh per financial year.

## 2. Employees' Provident Fund (EPF)
- **Eligibility**: Mandatory for salaried employees in registered establishments with basic salary > ₹15,000.
- **Contribution**: 12% of basic + DA by employee, matched by employer.

## 3. National Pension System (NPS)
- **Structure**: Market-linked voluntary retirement scheme managed by PFRDA.
- **Tax Benefit**: Additional ₹50,000 deduction under Section 80CCD(1B) over and above Section 80C limit.
- **Maturity**: 60% tax-free lump sum; 40% must be used to purchase an annuity.
