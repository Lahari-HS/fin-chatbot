# Mutual Funds vs. Fixed Deposits (FD)

Last reviewed: 2026-11-01
Note: Past performance of mutual funds does not guarantee future results.

## Comparison
| Metric | Fixed Deposit (FD) | Mutual Funds (Equity/Hybrid) |
|---|---|---|
| **Returns** | Fixed, guaranteed by bank | Market-linked, variable |
| **Risk** | Very low (DICGC insured up to ₹5 lakh) | Moderate to High |
| **Inflation Protection** | Low (real returns often post-tax 0-1%) | High (historically beats inflation over 7+ years) |
| **Liquidity** | Premature penalty applies | High (redeemable in 1-3 business days) |
| **Taxation** | Taxed at your income tax slab rate | Equity: 12.5% LTCG above ₹1.25L, 20% STCG |

## Ideal Use Case
- Use **FDs** for emergency funds and goals due within 1–3 years.
- Use **Mutual Funds** for long-term wealth creation (5+ years).
