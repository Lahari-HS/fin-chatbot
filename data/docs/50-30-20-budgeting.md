# 50/30/20 Budgeting Rule

Last reviewed: 2026-11-01
Note: Useful framework for beginners; adjust ratios based on personal circumstances.

## Overview
Popularized by Senator Elizabeth Warren, the 50/30/20 rule divides your take-home (after-tax) monthly income into three simple buckets:

1. **50% for Needs**: Absolute essentials required to survive and maintain work. Includes rent, home loan EMI, groceries, utilities, basic insurance, and minimum debt payments.
2. **30% for Wants**: Discretionary expenses that enhance life quality but are not vital. Includes dining out, entertainment, vacations, gadgets, and subscription services.
3. **20% for Savings & Debt Acceleration**: Investments (SIP, PPF, emergency fund) and aggressive repayment of high-interest debt beyond minimums.

If your fixed needs exceed 50%, look for ways to cut discretionary spending or increase secondary income streams.
