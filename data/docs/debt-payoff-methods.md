# Debt Payoff Strategies: Avalanche vs. Snowball

Last reviewed: 2026-11-01
Note: Choose the strategy that aligns best with your psychological motivation and cash flow.

## 1. Debt Avalanche (Mathematically Optimal)
- **Method**: Pay minimums on all obligations, then channel every extra rupee into the debt with the **highest interest rate** (e.g., credit cards at 36-42% APR).
- **Advantage**: Saves the highest amount of total interest and clears debt fastest in absolute numbers.

## 2. Debt Snowball (Psychologically Motivating)
- **Method**: Pay minimums on all obligations, then channel extra cash into the debt with the **smallest balance**, regardless of interest rate.
- **Advantage**: Provides rapid psychological "wins" by extinguishing accounts quickly, building behavioral momentum.

Both strategies rely on rolling over freed-up minimum payments into the next target debt until all debts are paid off.
