# Term Insurance Basics

Last reviewed: 2026-11-01
Note: Insurance is for risk protection, not wealth creation. Never mix investment and insurance (avoid endowment/ULIPs).

## What is Term Insurance?
Term insurance is pure life cover. If the policyholder passes away during the policy tenure, the nominee receives the sum assured. If the policyholder survives the term, there is no maturity payout.

## How Much Cover Do You Need?
- **Standard Rule of Thumb**: 10 to 15 times your annual income, PLUS outstanding liabilities (home loans, car loans).
- **Tenure**: Choose cover until your expected retirement age (e.g., age 60–65), when dependents are self-sufficient.
